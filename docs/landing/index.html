POI · PROOF OF INSIGHT

Lock your market call before the outcome.

Write down what you think will happen, and fix it on-chain while the answer is still unknown. When the window closes, a smart contract decides whether you were right — not you. You cannot edit it. You cannot delete it. Being wrong stays on the record too.

Why this is hard to fake

The window can't be backdated

The observation window must start after you commit. You cannot pick a period that already happened and claim you called it.

You don't decide the result

The contract recomputes the outcome from the observed value. If your submission disagrees with the arithmetic, the transaction reverts.

Silence is visible

Declare an outcome and never post the result, and the record shows overdue. There is no quiet way out.

How it works

  1. CommitYour reasoning goes up as a hash. What you'll measure — metric, threshold, window — is declared in the open, right then.
  2. The window closesThe observed value is derived from public data, so anyone can recompute it independently.
  3. The contract settles itRight or wrong is arithmetic, not opinion. A third party can dispute the observation on-chain, and you cannot erase that dispute.

Where it stands today

This is a testnet prototype, and it has no users yet. One person built it. What exists is real and verifiable — deployed contracts with verified source, a public interface that opens without a wallet, and on-chain records you can inspect right now, including a settled call and a third-party dispute. What does not exist yet is anyone using it besides the author.
